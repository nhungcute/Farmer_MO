# MO_FARM_INTERNAL_ASSET_POLICY_V1

## Scope

This project asset policy applies only to artwork whose provenance is explicitly recorded as:

```text
source=internal-generated
```

and which has been included in the relevant Project Owner approval scope. It currently covers the 112 Wave 1 assets: Chicken (92), Rice (5), Carrot (5), Corn (5), and Tomato (5).

This policy is a project asset-use policy. It is not a source-code, dependency, third-party asset, marketplace asset, font, library, or provider license.

## Permitted project use

The Project Owner permits artwork covered by this policy to be:

- used in Mỡ Farm;
- edited;
- resized;
- cropped by the project asset pipeline;
- animated;
- packed into texture atlases;
- bundled in project builds;
- distributed as part of Mỡ Farm;
- used in Mỡ Farm screenshots, trailers, and store material.

## Derivatives

Derivatives created for Mỡ Farm may continue to be managed under this policy when their provenance is retained and they remain within the approved project scope.

## Third-party exclusion

This policy does not apply to dependencies, third-party assets, marketplace assets, fonts, libraries, or source material that carries its own license. Those materials retain and follow their original license terms.

## Provider and tool boundary

This policy does not claim rights beyond the rights permitted by the applicable generation tool or provider terms and policies. It does not state that the project owns all copyrights worldwide without separate legal evidence or a legal basis.

## Redistribution boundary

The policy permits distributing covered artwork as part of Mỡ Farm. It does not grant a third party permission to extract the raw artwork and redistribute it as an independent asset pack.

## Approval authority

The Project Owner is the approval authority for this project asset policy and for release approval of assets covered by it.

## Policy identifier

Canonical manifest value:

```text
license=MO_FARM_INTERNAL_ASSET_POLICY_V1
```
